MySkin AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MySkin AI ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the MySkin AI mobile application.

MySkin AI allows users to create, edit, import, export, and generate Minecraft-compatible character skins.

1. Information We Process

MySkin AI does not require users to create an account and does not directly request information such as your name, contacts, precise location, microphone data, or camera data.

Depending on the features you use, the following information may be processed:

  • An anonymous installation identifier generated by MySkin AI.
  • Text prompts and technical generation information when you use the AI skin-generation feature.
  • Advertising-related information processed by Google Mobile Ads, which may include device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, diagnostics, and related technical information.
  • Purchase information processed by Google Play and RevenueCat when you make an in-app purchase.
  • Technical network information necessary for communication with online services.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

MySkin AI stores certain information locally in the application's private storage.

This may include:

  • Your skin projects and 64×64 skin textures.
  • Project names and model type.
  • Project timestamps and source information.
  • Recovery copies used to reduce accidental data loss.
  • Your most recently opened project.
  • Recently used colors.
  • Language preferences.
  • Ad-Free Pass expiry information.
  • An anonymous installation identifier.

Creating, editing, or saving a skin locally does not automatically upload the skin to our servers.

Clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application will normally remove information stored in the application's private storage, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

PNG files that you export outside the application's private storage remain in the location you selected until you delete them.

3. AI Skin Generation

When you use the AI generation feature, MySkin AI sends information required to generate the skin to our backend infrastructure.

This includes:

  • The text prompt you enter.
  • The selected skin model type.
  • A recipe version.
  • A generation request identifier.
  • Your anonymous installation identifier.

This information is sent securely over HTTPS to a Supabase Edge Function.

The anonymous installation identifier is used for purposes such as enforcing AI usage limits and preventing abuse. It is not created by MySkin AI for advertising tracking.

Our backend sends the prompt and the instructions required for generation to the OpenAI API. OpenAI returns structured information used by MySkin AI to create the final 64×64 skin.

MySkin AI does not currently send your imported PNG files or locally edited skin textures to OpenAI.

You should not include personal, confidential, financial, medical, or otherwise sensitive information in AI prompts.

4. Advertising

MySkin AI uses Google Mobile Ads to display advertising.

The application may contain:

  • Banner advertisements.
  • Interstitial advertisements.
  • Rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may process information such as IP address, advertising identifiers, app interactions, device information, diagnostic information, and advertising activity for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement, reporting, and fraud prevention.

Where required, MySkin AI uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide advertising privacy and consent choices before ads are requested.

Depending on your region and privacy choices, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted advertising.

Where required by Google UMP, the application provides a Privacy Options entry point that allows you to review or change your advertising privacy choices.

5. Ad-Free Pass

MySkin AI may offer a temporary Ad-Free Pass through an in-app purchase.

While the Ad-Free Pass is active, banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements associated with Save, Import, and Export actions are suppressed.

AI generation may still require viewing a rewarded advertisement.

6. In-App Purchases

MySkin AI integrates Google Play Billing and RevenueCat for in-app purchase functionality.

When purchase functionality is used, information such as the following may be processed by Google Play and RevenueCat:

  • Product identifiers.
  • Purchase status.
  • Transaction and purchase history.
  • App and device information required for purchase processing.
  • An anonymous MySkin AI installation identifier.

MySkin AI may provide the anonymous installation identifier to RevenueCat as an App User ID.

MySkin AI does not receive or store your complete payment-card details. Payments are handled by Google Play and its payment providers.

7. Anonymous Installation Identifier

MySkin AI generates a random UUID on your device.

This identifier may be used for:

  • AI generation quota enforcement.
  • Request integrity and abuse prevention.
  • RevenueCat purchase-state identification.

This identifier is separate from Google's advertising identifier and is not created by MySkin AI for cross-app advertising or tracking.

The identifier can remain across app sessions. Clearing application data or reinstalling the application will normally cause a new identifier to be generated.

8. Imported and Exported Images

When you import a PNG skin, the operating system allows you to select the file that MySkin AI should access.

The selected image is read and validated locally by the application.

Imported skins and locally edited skin textures are not currently uploaded to Supabase or OpenAI.

When exporting a skin, MySkin AI creates a 64×64 PNG file and uses the system's file-saving functionality.

Files exported outside MySkin AI's private application storage remain under your control until you delete them.

9. Third-Party Services

MySkin AI uses third-party services that may process information according to their own privacy policies.

These services include:

  • Google Mobile Ads — advertising, measurement, diagnostics, and fraud prevention.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — advertising consent and privacy choices.
  • Google Play Billing — Android in-app purchase processing.
  • RevenueCat — purchase and transaction infrastructure.
  • Supabase — backend services, Edge Functions, and AI-generation quota information.
  • OpenAI API — AI-assisted generation of structured skin information.

10. Data Retention

Local projects and preferences remain on your device until they are deleted, application data is cleared, or the application is uninstalled, subject to operating-system backup behavior.

Exported files remain in the location where they were saved until you delete them.

AI quota information associated with an anonymous installation identifier may be stored in our Supabase backend for the operation, security, and abuse-prevention needs of the AI-generation service.

Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to those providers' policies and applicable legal requirements.

11. Data Security

MySkin AI uses HTTPS when communicating with its backend infrastructure.

Skin projects stored locally by the application are kept in app-private storage where supported by the operating system.

Private backend credentials, including OpenAI API credentials and Supabase service credentials, are not embedded in the Flutter application.

No electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

MySkin AI does not intentionally ask users to provide their name, email address, or other direct personal information when using the core skin editor.

Parents or guardians who believe that personal information relating to a child has been provided through the application may contact us using the details below.

13. Your Choices

Depending on your device, region, and the features you use, you may:

  • Review advertising privacy choices through the application's Privacy Options feature when available.
  • Manage or reset advertising identifiers through your Android device settings where supported.
  • Choose whether to use optional AI-generation features.
  • Choose whether to make an in-app purchase.
  • Delete locally saved skin projects.
  • Clear MySkin AI's local application data through your device settings.
  • Uninstall MySkin AI.
  • Contact us regarding privacy or data-deletion questions.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if MySkin AI's functionality, service providers, legal requirements, or data-processing practices change.

The date displayed at the top of this page indicates when the policy was last updated.
